**SOME CLUBS ETC... WHERE MEETINGS ARE HELD**

Clubs are not A.A. Clubs are facilities rented by A.A. members for the purpose of holding multiple meetings. In keeping with A.A.’s primary purpose, Club Listings are provided and may be of interest to the recovering alcoholic.  
In keeping with our A.A. Traditions, A.A. does _not_ endorse, finance or accept contributions from these clubs.

**[A.A. Guideline: Relationship Between A.A. and Clubs](http://www.aa.org/lang/en/en_pdfs/mg-03_clubs.pdf)**

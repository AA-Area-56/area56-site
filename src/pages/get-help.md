---
title: Get help
---
If you think you may have a problem with alcohol, you're not alone. Alcoholics Anonymous is free, and anyone who wants to stop drinking is welcome. The quickest way to start is to go to a meeting or call your local intergroup's phone line.

## Find a meeting

- **Near Cincinnati:** [Greater Cincinnati Intergroup](https://aacincinnati.org) lists local meetings and has a phone line.
- **Near Dayton:** [Dayton Area Intergroup](https://aadaytononline.org) lists local meetings and has a phone line.
- **On your phone:** the free [Meeting Guide app](https://www.aa.org/meeting-guide-app) finds meetings near you.
- **Online:** [Online Intergroup of A.A.](https://aa-intergroup.org) lists meetings you can join from anywhere.

## Open and closed meetings

Open meetings are available to anyone interested in Alcoholics Anonymous, including family, friends, and professionals. Closed meetings are for A.A. members, or for anyone who has a drinking problem and has a desire to stop drinking.
